Khaleja[a] (transl.Courage)[3] is a 2010 Indian Telugu-language fantasy
action comedy film written and directed by Trivikram Srinivas. It stars Mahesh Babu and Anushka Shetty, alongside
Prakash Raj, Rao Ramesh, Shafi, Sunil, Ali, and Subbaraju in supporting roles. The music was composed by Mani Sharma.
The film follows Raju, a taxi driver who is unexpectedly hailed as the prophesied divine saviour of a village
suffering from a mysterious disease and must come to terms with his newfound status to protect the villagers.
